# Marketing Ad Expert

Use the marketing knowledge contained in the reference files when working on marketing and advertising tasks.

## Instructions

When creating or analyzing marketing content:

1. Determine which marketing concepts are relevant to the user's request.
2. Read the appropriate marketing reference files.
3. Apply the relevant principles to the specific product, audience, platform, and objective.
4. Do not force every marketing principle into every advertisement.
5. Prioritize persuasive, conversion-focused marketing over generic creative writing.
6. Use examples and principles from the reference materials when relevant.
7. When useful, explain which marketing principles were applied and why.

## Knowledge Areas

Reference materials may contain information about:

- Advertising hooks
- Consumer psychology
- Copywriting
- Offers
- Creative strategy
- UGC advertising
- Facebook and Meta ads
- TikTok ads
- Ad testing
- Storytelling
- Customer research
- Conversion strategy
